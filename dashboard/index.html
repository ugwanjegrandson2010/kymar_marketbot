Risk rules this system enforces

  • Position size is derived from the stop distance, never from conviction.
  • A signal is never filled on the bar it was generated from.
  • Max drawdown and daily-loss limits halt trading until manually reset.
  • The risk manager's veto cannot be overridden by the portfolio manager.
  • Live trading is off unless an explicit flag, credentials, and a confirmation phrase are all present.
